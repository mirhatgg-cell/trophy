# Original material maps

22 authored material profiles, each supplied as a 512×512 albedo, tangent-space normal and roughness map. Generated deterministically by `python3 tools/museum_textures.py`; no third-party photographs or catalog textures are copied.

Profiles: wet mud, clay, quarried stone, copper, bronze, silver, gold, ruby, emerald, sapphire, amethyst, obsidian, ice, plasma, starlight, comet, cosmos, rift, eternity, legend, ghost and galaxy.

The ready Roblox place uses its native textured PBR materials and actual relief geometry. These PNGs are a separate original texture export. They are not claimed to be uploaded Roblox assets. Applying them as custom images requires authorized upload to the owning experience and real asset IDs. No invented `rbxassetid` placeholders are shipped in the place.

Mud has wet/dry roughness regions and an irregular height field. Gold/silver/copper/bronze have directional brush grooves; copper and bronze include patina. Ghost is white/cyan mist, while galaxy is a dark blue spiral/star field. Their data is different, not one hue-shifted image.
